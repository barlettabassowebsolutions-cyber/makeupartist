Nice to meet you. I’m Shruti.

I’ve loved makeup since I was a kid. I started freelancing in 2009, then joined MAC Cosmetics and became a manager there.

Since then I’ve worked on every skin tone and type, from natural looks to full drama and even some FX. Today I’m a beauty stylist and run my own bridal business from Bellmore, traveling to brides across Long Island, New York City and the boroughs.

FAQs

How much does bridal makeup cost?

Traditional bridal makeup starts at $225, with a $150 trial. Airbrush bridal makeup starts at $250, with a $175 trial. Bridesmaids, mothers and family start at $125 per person. Send your date and party size for an exact quote.

Do you do hair too?

Yes. Shruti offers bridal makeup and hair, and a stylist from her team can help when your party is large.

Do you come to us?

Yes. Shruti works on site at your home, hotel or venue across Long Island, New York City and the boroughs.

What’s included?

False lashes and a consultation come with every makeup booking.

How big a bridal party can you do?

Up to 20 people on the wedding day.

Which dates are you booking?

Now booking 2027 and 2028 brides, with limited availability left in 2026.

Can you work with my skin, allergies or hooded eyes?

Yes. Years at MAC Cosmetics mean Shruti has worked with every skin tone and type. Past brides mention her care with allergies, hooded and uneven lids, and keeping makeup natural for brides who rarely wear it.

Will my makeup last all night?

It’s built to. Past brides say theirs held up through humidity, pouring rain and a hurricane weekend.
